Protect your products with robust materials

Your custom mailer box is constructed from corrugated cardboard, available in kraft, white, or a white-brown combination. For enhanced durability, options include corrugated bleached cardboard and corrugated cardboard coated. This range of materials, with grammages from 358 gsm to 510 gsm, provides a sturdy enclosure for your items during transit, ensuring they arrive securely.

Create a memorable unboxing experience

Customise the interior of your mailer box to extend your brand's presence. With print coverage options for the inside, outside, or both, you can apply your design across all visible surfaces. Choose between multicolour muted, saturated, or premium printing to match your brand's aesthetic, making every unboxing a distinctive moment for your customers.

Sizes to fit your specific needs

Available in small, medium, and large, these mailer boxes are designed to accommodate a variety of products. Each box maintains a dimensional tolerance of ±2-4mm, ensuring a consistent fit for your items. Furthermore, all sizes are engineered to fit standard courier and parcel-locker dimensions, simplifying your shipping logistics.

Enhance your design with professional finishes

Apply a glossy or matte foil finish to your mailer box to give it a distinct look. These finishes add a layer of visual texture and can highlight specific elements of your design, creating a polished presentation.

Secure your contents with reliable closures

For added security and convenience, custom mailer boxes can be equipped with a single or double adhesive strip. This feature provides a strong seal for transit and offers an easy-open solution for recipients.

Material

  • Corrugated cardboardDurable and lightweight, composed of layers of paper connected by a corrugated insert. Excellent cushioning and crush resistance.
  • Corrugated bleached cardboardA bright, elegant surface with high crush resistance — accepts print very well, for high colour intensity.
  • Corrugated cardboard, coatedA protective surface layer improves resistance to moisture, dirt and mechanical damage during transport.

Print colour mode

  • One ColourA single CMYK colour keeps costs low — perfect for minimalist designs.
  • White ColourWhite ink via UV printing, ideal for strong contrast on kraft cardboard.
  • Multicolour – saturatedAffordable multicolour printing on bleached cardboard with good colour accuracy.
  • Multicolour – premiumOur top-tier print on coated cardboard — sharp, high-definition results, even for photographic imagery.

Print coverage

  • OutsidePrint is made on the outside of the box.
  • Outside + InsidePrint is made on both the outside and the inside.
  • InsidePrint is made on the inside only.

Adhesive strip

  • NoneThe box closes with tucked flaps only, no adhesive strip.
  • Single adhesive stripA secure closure for single-use shipping.
  • Double adhesive stripA second strip makes returns easy — reseal securely for send-back items.

Foil

  • Glossy foilAn intense shine that boosts contrast and highlights key elements of your design.
  • Matte foilA subtle, gloss-free finish for a refined, premium look.
